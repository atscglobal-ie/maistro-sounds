# mAIstro studio pack: notices

Every instrument in this pack is built from a sample library released under the Creative Commons
CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
CC0 asks for no attribution; we credit the authors anyway. The samples were trimmed, premixed
(drum and piano microphones), resampled where a kit mixed sample rates, and re-encoded as FLAC.
